This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Zart Zurt. Our app does not require any registration or login.

🛡️

No Personal Data Collection

Zart Zurt does not collect any personal information from its users. There is no registration, no login, no account creation. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other personal data. Your privacy is fully respected.

🎭 1. Application Overview

Zart Zurt is a free entertainment application that provides:

  • Funny cartoon characters and animations
  • Troll sounds and sound effects
  • Shake-to-change voice pitch & sound effect feature
  • Joke and prank content for entertainment
  • A fun, lighthearted experience with no competitive or social features

The application is designed purely for entertainment purposes. It does not require any user account, login, or registration to use. All features are available without providing any personal information.

🔒 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be completely transparent: Zart Zurt does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • User accounts, passwords, or personal login credentials
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Health, fitness, or physical activity tracking data
  • Contacts, personal photos, or private files from your device
  • Messages, call logs, or browsing history
  • Credit card, bank account, or payment credentials (all payments are processed directly by Google Play)

🎯 Minimal Data Principle

Zart Zurt does not require registration or personal user profile creation. We only store an anonymous FCM device token for sending notifications and verifying subscription access via Google Play Store.

📲 3. Device Sensors & Shake Detection (Voice-Changing Feature)

Zart Zurt utilizes your device's built-in motion sensors (accelerometer) strictly to detect when the device is shaken in order to trigger the interactive voice-changing sound feature.

⚠️ Important Declaration for Google Play & App Store Compliance

This app is for entertainment only. It uses the accelerometer/shake detection for a voice-changing feature. It is NOT a health or fitness app and does not track physical activity.

Motion and accelerometer data are evaluated strictly in real time on your local device to trigger sound effects. No sensor data or motion history is stored, recorded, analyzed, or transmitted to any external server or third party.

🔧 4. Third-Party Services & RevenueCat Integration

Our application utilizes third-party SDKs and services for core functionality, audio hosting, subscription management, notifications, and analytics:

Google Play Services & Google Play Billing

Used for app distribution, system integration, and processing optional monthly subscription packages. All in-app purchase transactions and payment billing are handled directly and securely by Google Play Billing. We do not process, collect, or store credit card details on our own servers. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions, verify active paid features, and validate purchase receipts provided by Google Play Billing / App Store. RevenueCat processes anonymous App User IDs and purchase status data strictly to grant subscription access. RevenueCat does not store or process raw financial or payment card details. For details, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Services (FCM Device Token & Storage)

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to maintain an anonymous device token strictly to deliver push notifications and app updates. We also use Firebase Storage to host and deliver our custom audio sound files to the app. No personal credentials, user profiles, or personal identity information are collected or stored in Firebase. For more information, see Firebase Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob

We use AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, including personalized ads based on user interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings. For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics and performance crash reports, helping us improve app performance and user experience. For more information, see Firebase Privacy Policy.

💳 5. Subscriptions, Payments & Advertisements

Zart Zurt offers both free ad-supported features and optional paid monthly subscription packages:

  • In-App Subscriptions (RevenueCat & Google Play): Optional monthly subscription packages are available to unlock premium sound features or ad-free experiences. Subscription billing and auto-renewals are handled securely through Google Play Store, and active subscription rights are validated via RevenueCat.
  • Advertisements (Google AdMob): Free users may see banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads. You can manage or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

🔐 6. Data Security

Although we do not collect personal data, we are committed to ensuring the security of our application. We implement the following measures:

  • All third-party SDKs used are from reputable, well-known providers (Google)
  • We regularly update our app and dependencies to address security vulnerabilities
  • The app does not request unnecessary device permissions
  • All network communications use encrypted (HTTPS/SSL) connections

👶 7. Children's Privacy

Zart Zurt is a fun entertainment app suitable for a general audience. However, the app is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. Since no registration or login is required, no personal data of any kind is collected from users of any age.

🎈 Family-Friendly Content

While Zart Zurt contains cartoon characters and fun sounds suitable for general audiences, parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor their children's app usage and manage ad preferences through device settings.

🔄 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, third-party services, or legal requirements. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy
  • Displaying an in-app notification for significant changes

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
